Add your own cover

Made a cover for a Kazeta cart? Here's how to add it to this library:

  1. Upload your image file to this repository, in the same folder as index.html.
  2. Open index.html, scroll to the covers list near the bottom of the file, and add one line with your image's filename and the game's title.
  3. Commit the change — the library updates itself automatically.
